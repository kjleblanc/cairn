## Visible outcome

The inline pause between Review and Start reads as one calm paper dispatch
checkpoint instead of a large rounded settings card. The final requested work
leads, its provenance remains easy to scan, and the next truthful state —
choosing a builder, connection needed, ready for an offline demonstration, or
ready for one real call — is unmistakable.

For a real worker, provider, model, target project, exact task, readable data,
cost/quota, and the one-call approval remain fully visible before Start can
unlock. Those facts use a ruled docket rather than a grid of little cards. The
native approval box is visually distinct from ordinary information without
becoming a chunky pill. Start and Cancel are quiet flat paper actions with
clear hierarchy and keyboard focus. Compact layouts wrap long model, project,
task, data, and cost text without clipping or horizontal travel.

The checkpoint belongs to the approved faded dusk paper system: slight paper
translucency, controlled rules, one restrained registration detail, pastel
cyan/amber used sparingly, modest corners, and no glass border, shadow, inflated
button, scribbled edge, decorative travel, or faux handwriting.
