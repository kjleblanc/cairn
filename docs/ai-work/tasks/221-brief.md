separately approved under the disclosed provider/model/payload/caps/billing
basis; the untouched preregistered set passes in full; the dated redacted
evaluation records exact secret-free fingerprints and supplied usage; only the
passing tuple is activated; exact-tuple task critic routing works under its own
fresh approval; the owner confirms the disclosure/result is understandable;
one report and one log row answer every check; the exact Task 221 paths land in
one local final commit; and `main` is clean.

or final judgment; a call/time/output cap would be exceeded; any held-out

or recovery is unclear. Activation stays empty and any prompt/fixture revision
